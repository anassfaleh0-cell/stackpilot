## 2. Prioritized Action Plan

### Wave 1 — Foundations & Quick Wins (Days 1–21)
**Goal: 25–35 submissions; 10–15 live links.**

1. **Claim the big-five review profiles** — G2, Capterra, GetApp, Software Advice, TrustRadius (requires starting the verified-review collection campaign from existing readers; 5+ reviews minimum).
2. **Complete brand footprint** — Crunchbase, Wellfound, LinkedIn Company, Clutch, Trustpilot, D&B, ZoomInfo, Sitejabber (citation value even where NoFollow).
3. **Batch-submit easy directories** — SaaSHub, SoftwareSuggest, SaaSworthy, Crozdesk, GoodFirms, F6S, StartupBlink, Startup Stash, Startup Ranking, Startups.fyi, The Org, BetaPage, Launching Next, Uneed.
4. **Submit AI directories** — There's An AI For That, Futurepedia, Future Tools, Toolify, TopAI.tools, Supertools, Insidr, Dang AI.
5. **Publish the first linkable asset** (Phase 4 idea #1 or #2) and run the dev-community sync: dev.to + Hashnode cross-posts with canonical tags.
6. **Launch stack page** on StackShare.

### Wave 2 — Outreach Engine (Days 22–60)
**Goal: 60–70 pitches sent; 25–40 live links.**

7. **Guest posts (Tier 1):** pitch SEJ, SEL, MarTech, CMI, Neil Patel with data-backed outlines from the first research reports; fill volume with SaaS Marketplace + IdeasPlusBusiness.
8. **Resource page outreach:** Template 2 to resource pages + universities (UC Davis, UCF, Clarkson) + Cayenne + startupresources.io + startuptools.co.
9. **Broken link building:** export competitor backlinks from Ahrefs → find 404s → Template 3 to ~20 webmasters/week.
10. **Journalist platform responses:** daily digests on Qwoted (free tier), Source of Sources (free, highest follow-link rate), and Featured/HARO; target ≥3 responses/week. Respond within 60 minutes of query.
11. **Founder interviews:** Starter Story, IdeaMensch, Failory, SaaS Club, How I Built It (Template 5).
12. **Podcasts:** The SaaS Podcast, Indie Hackers, The Bootstrapped Founder first (bookable), then SaaStr (Template 6).

### Wave 3 — Amplification & Long Tail (Days 61–90)
**Goal: 80–100 total submissions/pitches; 50+ live links.**

13. **Launch week:** Product Hunt launch (with BetaList, Launching Next, Uneed, and a Show HN data story timed around it).
14. **Newsletter + roundup push:** TLDR, SaaStr Daily, SaaS Weekly, TLDR Marketing, The Rundown AI, Ben's Bites, Growth Unhinged.
15. **Publish remaining linkable assets** (20 total from Phase 4) and re-pitch each to journalists via Template 8.
16. **Link reclamation:** weekly brand-mention scan (Google Alerts + Ahrefs) → Template 7.
17. **Open-source communities:** awesome-list PRs (GitHub), SourceForge if a free tool exists; fosstodon research threads.
18. **University + residual targets:** complete remaining Long-tail submissions.
