## Post 2 — Data Thread (category: "Show and tell")

**Title:** We tested 150+ tools hands-on. Here's what we found (methodology + data)

**Body:**

> Dataset: user feedback aggregated from public review platforms across 12 software categories, cross-checked against our hands-on tests.
>
> **Three findings:**
> 1. Review specificity predicts product quality — vague reviews cluster around weak products.
> 2. Support-related reviews correlate with overall ratings more than feature reviews.
> 3. "Easy to use" is the most common pro AND con — ease-of-use is audience-dependent.
>
> Full writeup + methodology: [pilotstack.online/blog/software-evaluation-playbook-2026](https://www.pilotstack.online/blog/software-evaluation-playbook-2026)
