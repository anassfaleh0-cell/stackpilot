## Profile Post (first post, content-first)

**Title:** I hands-on tested 150+ tools. Here are 3 patterns product people should know.

**Body:**

> I run PilotStack, an independent software review platform — we test every tool hands-on for two weeks before rating it.
>
> Three patterns from testing 150+ tools hands-on across 12 software categories:
>
> 1. **Reviews with specific behavior details are 3x more useful** than generic praise — and they correlate with honest products. Train your eye to skip vague five-star love.
> 2. **Support experience predicts overall satisfaction better than features.** In every category we measured, support-related reviews had the strongest correlation with a product's final score.
> 3. **"Easy to use" appears in both pros AND cons** of the same product categories — ease-of-use is contextual. A tool that's easy for one team is a maze for another.
>
> Full methodology is free on our site ([PilotStack](https://www.pilotstack.online) → Methodology). What patterns have you noticed in user feedback for your own product?
