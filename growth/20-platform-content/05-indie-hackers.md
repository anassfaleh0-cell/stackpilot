## Post 1 — Milestone / Story Post (post on a growth day, e.g., after PH launch)

**Title:** We built a software review platform that refuses vendor money — 1.1M words of research later

**Body:**

> Two years ago we started PilotStack because we were sick of review sites that let vendors pay for placement. Our premise: test every tool hands-on for two weeks, score it on five transparent dimensions, publish everything free.
>
> What happened since:
> - 150+ tools reviewed hands-on
> - 600+ side-by-side comparisons published
> - 12 software categories, every score cross-checked against public review feedback
> - 1.1M words of research, 695+ pages
> - zero paid acquisition since day one
>
> The hardest part wasn't the writing — it was the discipline. No vendor can pay us. Ever. That constraint is our entire moat: readers know our scores aren't for sale.
>
> [pilotstack.online](https://www.pilotstack.online) — happy to answer anything about the model, the methodology, or what we learned along the way.

**Link placement:** branded anchor `pilotstack.online` in body (IH allows this; it's the product's home).
