## Launch Draft

**Name:** PilotStack
**Tagline (≤60 chars):** Independent software reviews & comparisons, tested hands-on
**Short description (≤135 chars):** Every tool tested 2 weeks on a transparent 5-dimension rubric. No vendor can pay for placement.

**Long description (PH limit ~260 chars in first section, full story after):**

> PilotStack is an independent software review platform. We test every tool hands-on for two weeks, score it on a published five-dimension rubric (features, ease of use, support, value, performance), and cross-check every score against user feedback from public review platforms.
>
> Unlike aggregate review sites, PilotStack accepts zero vendor money for ratings. Our editorial independence policy is published, our methodology is open, and our scorecard is free to steal.
>
> **What's inside:**
> - 100+ in-depth reviews with pros/cons and real-world use cases
> - 600+ side-by-side comparisons
> - 100+ buying guides and selection checklists
> - 130+ research & statistics pages
> - Free selection scorecard template
>
> We founded PilotStack in 2024 because software buying had become a weeks-long research project with no trusted source. Today: 695+ pages, 1.1M+ words, 150+ hands-on reviews, and a scorecard that isn't for sale.

**First comment (posted by founder immediately after launch):**

> Hi Hunters — I'm [Name], founder of PilotStack. Two years ago I watched a team buy a $60k/year tool that died in 6 months because the reviews they trusted were marketing. So we built the review platform we wished existed: every tool tested hands-on for two weeks, scored on a public rubric, no vendor money.
>
> Ask me anything about testing 150+ tools hands-on, or why we publish our scorecard for free. Also happy to review your stack!
