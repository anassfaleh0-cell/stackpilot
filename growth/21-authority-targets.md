## Verification Checklist (per target, before submitting)

1. **DR is real** — re-check with Ahrefs/Moz; adjust tier if moved >10 points.
2. **Link type confirmed** — dofollow claimed? verify on a live profile of a similar site, or accept NF where noted.
3. **Category fit** — the site accepts software-review/research platforms; no forced angles.
4. **No noindex** — listing/profile pages are indexable.
5. **No paid path used** — free submission or earned editorial only (E-tier rules).
6. **Asset ready** — for A/B editorial targets, the flagship research asset (Phase 5) must be live and citable first.
