> Cold-email and in-platform templates for every link acquisition tactic in the Sprint 19 engine. Complements `growth/08-outreach-templates.md`; this kit is the 2026 refresh with HARO-era platforms and listing-specific templates.

## Follow-Up Sequence

**Follow-up 1 (day 3):**
> Subject: Re: [Original Subject]
> Hi [First Name], just bumping this to the top of your inbox — I know things get busy. [2-sentence reminder of the offer]. Happy to make it easy on your end.

**Follow-up 2 (day 7):** Short version of follow-up 1 with a new supporting asset (e.g., "a link to the draft outline / a data chart").

**Follow-up 3 (day 14, final):** Close politely: "If this isn't a fit, no worries at all — appreciate your time either way." Then move on.

**Compliance:** include your postal address + unsubscribe line in all emails (CAN-SPAM); respect GDPR opt-out.
