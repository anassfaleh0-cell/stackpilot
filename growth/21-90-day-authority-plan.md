# Phase 13 — 90-Day Authority Plan

> Sprint 21 · Execution plan that converts the audit (Phase 1), target list (Phase 3), flagship (Phase 5), PR kit (Phase 6), contributions (Phase 7), anchor strategy (Phase 8), and internal map (Phase 9) into a 90-day cadence. Realistic goals based on the current profile (DR ≈ 0.2, ~1 live link): **authority is built through Tier A/B editorial, not directory volume.**

---

## 1. Goals (90 days)

| KPI | Conservative | Realistic | Stretch | Source |
|-----|--------------|-----------|---------|--------|
| External referring domains (new, distinct) | 8 | 15 | 22 | Ahrefs / tracker |
| Tier A/B live links | 4 | 8 | 12 | Tracker (`21-authority-targets.md`) |
| Tier C/D live links (batch) | 4 | 7 | 10 | Tracker |
| Branded search impressions growth | +25% | +50% | +100% | GSC |
| Editorial pitches sent (Tier A/B) | 20 | 30 | 40 | Tracker |
| Flagship citations (mentions with link) | 3 | 6 | 10 | Ahrefs content explorer / brand scan |
| Review profiles claimed (G2/Capterra/GetApp/SoftwareAdvice/TrustRadius) | 2 | 3 | 5 | Platforms |
| Verified user reviews submitted | 3 | 5 | 8 | Platforms (gate for Tier-A review platforms) |

**Honest caveat:** DR is a third-party metric with a 6–8 week lag; 90 days may show zero DR movement even with perfect execution. Primary KPIs are **referring domains and Tier A/B link count**, which drive DR later.

## 3. Weekly Routine (30 min)

1. Tracker review: statuses, follow-up dates (`growth/19-backlink-tracking.csv`)
2. Run follow-up sequence from `growth/17-outreach-kit.md`
3. Refresh brand-mention alerts (Google Alerts + Ahrefs)
4. Log every new link URL + anchor text (feeds `21-anchor-strategy.md` ratios)
5. Reply to 3 HARO-style queries/week (PR-3 template)

---

## 4. Monthly Review (60 min)

1. Count live referring domains vs. goals table (§1)
2. Check anchor ratios (branded ≥ 45%, exact ≤ 8%)
3. Review which Tier A/B targets responded; adjust pitch angles
4. Re-verify next wave targets' DR (audit checklist item 1)
5. Update `growth/21-authority-tracker.md` dashboard (Phase 14)

---

## 5. Rules (reaffirmed)

- No paid links (TLDR/Lenny's sponsorships are E-tier)
- No fabricated statistics in any pitch — all numbers trace to `growth/21-pricing-benchmark-data.json`
- No mass guest posting — max 1 contribution per publication per 60 days
- Every external link to a real, live page (flagship, methodology, or review)
- Log everything; quarterly anchor re-audit
