## 4. Long Company Description (1,200–1,500 characters)

> PilotStack is an independent software review and research platform that helps businesses choose the right software, every time.
>
> Founded in 2024, PilotStack was built on a simple observation: the software landscape has become overwhelming. Thousands of new tools launch every year, buying decisions that once took days now take weeks of research, and most review sites recycle vendor marketing instead of telling buyers the truth. PilotStack exists to change that.
>
> Unlike aggregate review sites, PilotStack tests every product hands-on for a minimum of two weeks using realistic business workflows. Each tool is scored across five equally weighted dimensions — Features, Ease of Use, Support, Value, and Performance — on a transparent 1–5 scale. Every review is independently verified by a second team member and cross-referenced against major review sources. We never accept payment for reviews, ratings, or placement. That is a policy, not a promise: our editorial independence is published, auditable, and enforced.
>
> Today, PilotStack publishes 695+ research pages — more than 1.1 million words — spanning 12 major software categories: CRM, project management, analytics, developer tools, AI, HR, marketing, and more. Our library includes 150+ in-depth tool reviews, 600+ side-by-side comparisons, audience-specific "best of" rankings, 100+ buying guides, industry pages, glossaries, and original research reports. Every score is cross-checked against user feedback aggregated from public review platforms, per our published methodology.
>
> PilotStack is free for all readers. We earn revenue only through clearly disclosed affiliate commissions and sponsored newsletter placements — never from influencing editorial content.
>

## 10. FAQs

**Q1: Is PilotStack free to use?**
A: Yes. All reviews, comparisons, and guides are free for every reader. PilotStack earns revenue only from clearly disclosed affiliate commissions and sponsored newsletter placements — never from influencing editorial content.

**Q2: How does PilotStack test software?**
A: Every tool is tested hands-on for a minimum of two weeks using realistic business workflows. Tools are scored across five equally weighted dimensions — Features, Ease of Use, Support, Value, Performance — on a 1–5 scale, and every review is independently verified by a second team member.

**Q3: Can vendors pay for positive reviews?**
A: No. PilotStack's editorial independence policy strictly prohibits paid placement, ratings, or coverage. This policy is published and enforced at pilotstack.online/editorial-independence.

**Q4: How many tools has PilotStack reviewed?**
A: 150+ in-depth reviews are live, supported by 600+ side-by-side comparisons, 100+ guides, and 130+ research and statistics pages spanning 12 software categories.

**Q5: Which software categories does PilotStack cover?**
A: 12 categories including CRM & Sales, Project Management, Marketing & SEO, Analytics & BI, AI & Machine Learning, Developer Tools, HR & Recruitment, Finance & Accounting, Customer Support, Communication, Security & Compliance, and Design & Creative.

**Q6: Who writes PilotStack reviews?**
A: A small, independent team that tests every tool hands-on per our published methodology. Individual bios are not published at this stage.

**Q7: How does PilotStack compare to G2 or Capterra?**
A: Unlike aggregate review platforms, PilotStack tests every product hands-on and publishes structured comparisons — it is research-first rather than crowd-sourced. We also reference and cross-check major review sources, but never pay for inclusion.

**Q8: Can I suggest a tool for PilotStack to review?**
A: Yes — submit suggestions via the contact page at pilotstack.online/contact.

**Q9: Is PilotStack suitable for my industry?**
A: Likely yes — we publish software recommendations for 50 industries and 49 use cases, from healthcare and legal to e-commerce and SaaS.

**Q10: Where can I read PilotStack's methodology?**
A: The full testing process, rubric, and scoring system is published at pilotstack.online/methodology and pilotstack.online/how-we-test-software.
