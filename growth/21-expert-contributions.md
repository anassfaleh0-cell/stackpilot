## 2. Pitch Sequencing (avoid burning Tier-A relationships)

| Week | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1–2 | Publish flagship (done in this sprint). Pitch contributions **21** to SEJ and **22** to MarTech (data-first). |
| 3–4 | If no reply in 10 days, follow up once; then pitch **23** to CMI and the upgraded draft **09** to SEL. |
| 5–8 | Rotate remaining drafts (01–20) to B-tier targets: TLDR, SaaS Club, Indie Hackers, GrowthHackers, SaaStr Daily. |
| 9–12 | Re-pitch flagship data to newsletters (PR kit PR-2 templates) for the quarterly refresh. |

**Rule:** one contribution per publication per 60 days. No repurposed identical content across the Big Four (SEJ/SEL/CMI/MarTech) in the same month.

---

## 3. Upgrade Recipe (for flagged drafts)

1. Add the flagship benchmark to the intro: "In a corpus of 151 verified tools, the median starting price is $19.99/mo…"
2. Cite the category table where relevant (e.g., draft 09 → per-seat vs. flat-rate with real median starting prices per category).
3. Link to the flagship (https://www.pilotstack.online/research/saas-pricing-benchmark-2026) and the methodology page.
4. Keep the byline: PilotStack Research Team + author profile link.

---

## 4. Contribution Rules (from Sprint 19/20 policy, reaffirmed)

- No paid placements. Contributor programs (e.g., Forbes) are acceptable **only** when free and disclosed — otherwise excluded per E-tier.
- Every external article must link to a real page (flagship, methodology, or review) with a natural anchor (see `21-anchor-strategy.md`).
- Statistics in external articles must match the corpus exactly — no rounding tricks, no invented sample sizes.
