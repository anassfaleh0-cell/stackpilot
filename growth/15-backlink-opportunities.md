## Sourcing Rules (non-negotiable)

1. **No paid links** — every target listed is earned via listing, review, content, or relationship. Where a "paid" tier exists (e.g., newsletter sponsorships), it is flagged and excluded from the acquisition plan unless approved separately.
2. **No PBNs or spam directories** — targets with high spam scores or "avoid" flags from the earlier audit (`growth/07-backlink-directories.md`) were excluded.
3. **Verification step** — before submitting each target, check: (a) DR is real, (b) site links out with DoFollow where claimed, (c) the site accepts your category, (d) no noindex on listing pages.
4. **Relevance over DA** — a DR 40 relevant resource page beats a DR 90 irrelevant one.
