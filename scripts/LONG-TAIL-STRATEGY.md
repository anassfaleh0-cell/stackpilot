## Content Priority Matrix

### Priority 1 — Write First (Month 1–2)
**Criteria:** High volume (>2,000) + Very low KD (<10) + Transactional intent

These pages are most likely to rank quickly and drive conversions:

1. **free AI tool for writing blog posts** — Huge volume, almost no competition
2. **best free project management software for startups** — High intent, easy win
3. **Notion alternative for personal knowledge management** — Branded competitor term
4. **ChatGPT alternative for coding free** — Trending topic, low KD
5. **best CRM for freelancers with no budget** — Perfect product-market fit keyword

### Priority 2 — Write Soon (Month 3–4)
**Criteria:** Medium volume (1,000–2,000) + Low KD (<15) + Informational/Commercial

These build topical authority and capture mid-funnel users:

1. **how to use AI for customer support** — Informational, showcases product use cases
2. **ChatGPT vs Claude for coding 2026** — Comparison content, high engagement potential
3. **free project management for remote teams under 10 people** — Niche-specific, high intent
4. **best AI writing tool for lawyers with small budget** — Vertical-specific, less competition
5. **how to use AI for email marketing campaigns** — Practical guide, drives tool adoption

### Priority 3 — Fill Gaps (Month 5–7)
**Criteria:** Lower volume (<1,000) + Very low KD (<8) + Specific niche

These are easy to rank and capture highly targeted traffic:

1. **CRM for personal trainers and coaches** — Ultra-niche, almost no competition
2. **project management tool for wedding planners** — Specific vertical, high conversion potential
3. **how to automate invoice reminders** — Utility content, builds trust
4. **CRM for consulting firms under $30/month** — Price-qualified traffic
5. **best tools for accounting firms** — B2B niche, high LTV customers

### Priority 4 — Evergreen Refresh (Month 8–10)
**Criteria:** Time-sensitive keywords that need annual updates

1. **free password manager comparison 2026** — Update yearly
2. **GitHub Copilot alternative open source** — Evolving market
3. **best IDE for React development 2026** — Annual refresh
4. **Mailchimp alternative with free plan 2026** — Competitive landscape changes
5. **free cloud hosting for side projects** — Pricing/features change often
